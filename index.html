Bookings, Deposits & Cancellation Policy

Please note that in case of more than three last minute cancellations, a €10 deposit will be required.

Your appointment is considered confirmed once the required deposit has been received.

The €10 deposit is applied toward the total cost of your treatment.

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please contact us as early as possible.

Late cancellations and missed appointments may result in the €10 deposit being non-refundable and a new deposit being required to make another booking.

This policy helps us protect appointment times reserved exclusively for each client and allows us to offer cancelled appointments to other clients.

By paying your deposit and confirming your appointment, you acknowledge and accept our booking and cancellation policy.
