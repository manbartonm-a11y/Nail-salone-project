Laser Repeat – 20-Day Policy

A laser repeat (touch-up) is available within 20 days of your original laser session and is intended only to check and treat any small areas where hairs may have been missed or have not responded as expected during the initial session.

The 20-day period is important because laser hair removal works according to the hair-growth cycle. After this window, we begin moving toward the next growth cycle and therefore toward your next regular laser appointment, rather than repeating the previous session.

The repeat is not a second full laser session. It is simply a touch-up of areas where necessary.

A repeat is completely optional and is not required. If the treated area is clear and you do not notice any remaining hairs that require a touch-up, there is no need to book one.

If you feel a repeat is necessary, please contact us within 20 days of your original appointment. After the 20-day period, the repeat is no longer valid and treatment will continue at your next scheduled laser session.

Bookings, Deposits & Cancellation Policy

A €10 deposit is required to secure your appointment.

Your appointment is considered confirmed once the required deposit has been received.

The €10 deposit is applied toward the total cost of your treatment.

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please contact us as early as possible.

Late cancellations and missed appointments may result in the €10 deposit being non-refundable and a new deposit being required to make another booking.

This policy helps us protect appointment times reserved exclusively for each client and allows us to offer cancelled appointments to other clients.

By paying your deposit and confirming your appointment, you acknowledge and accept our booking and cancellation policy.

Online Payments

For selected services and appointments, online payment is available.

Depending on the booking, you may:

  • Pay the required deposit online
  • Pay the full treatment amount online
  • Pay part of the amount online and the remaining balance at Nataly Laser House

If you would prefer to split your payment, please contact us before completing your booking so our team can guide you through the available payment options.
